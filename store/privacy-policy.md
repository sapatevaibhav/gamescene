# GameScene Studio privacy policy

*Last updated: 15 November 2026. Applies to GameScene Studio 0.23 and later on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux.*

GameScene Studio is an offline app. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no online features, and it never connects to the internet.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. No one, including the developers, can see what you make in it.

## Feedback, web links and licence files

- **Feedback email:** Tapping **Send feedback** opens your device's default mail application with an email addressed to `contact@ekaai.tech` pre-filled with technical details about your device (device model, operating system, GPU name and selected graphics tier) to help diagnose issues. The email is only sent if you choose to send it from your mail app, and you can edit or delete any details beforehand.
- **Web links:** Links in Settings and Credits (such as links to open-source licences, ekaai.tech, and ambientCG) open in your web browser. The app itself never connects to the internet.
- **Licence files:** If you install an optional `.gslicence` file, it is stored locally on your device in the app's own storage. It is never transmitted.

## What stays on your device

Everything you make stays on your device, in places you control:

- **Scenes** are saved in the app's own storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them unless you exported them first.
- **Videos and screenshots** you export are saved to your device's Videos/Movies and Pictures folders (on phones, your gallery or photo library).
- **Imported files** (3D models, objects, sprites, sounds, animation clips) are copied into the app's own storage, and are put inside a scene file when you export that scene.
- **Licence files** remain on your device in the app's private data.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why | When |
|---|---|---|
| Photo library: add only (iOS) | To save the videos and pictures you export to Photos | Asked the first time you save to the gallery |
| Storage (Android 7 to 9 only) | To save exported videos and pictures to the gallery | Asked the first time you export. Android 10 and later need no permission. |

The app doesn't use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or network.

## Sharing

When you tap **Share**, the system share sheet opens and you choose where the file goes. The app only hands over the file you picked; it doesn't see what the other app does with it.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will ship with an app update and be published at the same address, with the date at the top updated.
