# Name and logo

The code of GameScene Studio is open source (see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE](NOTICE)). The name and the artwork that identify it are not.

"GameScene Studio", the GameScene logo, the app icon, the wordmark and the splash image (everything in `assets/brand/`) belong to Vaibhav Sapate. All rights are reserved, and the GPL does not cover them.

## What you can do without asking

- Say that your project is "based on GameScene Studio" or "a fork of GameScene Studio". The licence asks you to say this.
- Use the name to refer to the original app in articles, videos, reviews and tutorials.
- Build the unmodified source for your own use, with the name and logo as they are.

## What needs a different name and artwork

- Distributing a modified version, whether as a download, on a store or on a website. Pick a name that can't be mistaken for GameScene Studio, and replace every file in `assets/brand/`, the app name in `project.godot` and `export_presets.cfg`, and the package id `com.gamescene.studio`.
- Publishing any build, changed or not, on an app store under the name GameScene Studio. Only the original author publishes under that name.

## What isn't allowed

- Suggesting that the original author made, approved or supports your version.
- Using the logo or name on merchandise, or as the name of a company, product or domain.

For anything else, ask: contact@ekaai.tech.
