Privacy

The app collects nothing.

Last updated 15 November 2026. Applies to GameScene Studio 0.23 and later on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux.

GameScene Studio is an offline app. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no online features, and it never connects to the internet. This website is separate from the app: it uses Google Tag Manager to count visits, which loads a script from Google and may set cookies in your browser. Nothing on the site reaches into the app.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. No one, including the developers, can see what you make in it.

Feedback, web links and licence files

  • Feedback email. Send feedback opens your own mail app with a message that lists your device model, operating system, GPU name and graphics tier. It is sent only if you send it, and you can edit it first.
  • Web links. Links in Settings and Credits open in your browser. The app itself never connects to the internet.
  • Licence files. An optional .gslicence file is stored on your device and never transmitted.

What stays on your device

  • Scenes are saved in the app's own storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them unless you exported them first.
  • Videos and screenshots go to your Videos and Pictures folders, or your gallery on a phone.
  • Imported files are copied into the app's own storage and placed inside a scene file when you export that scene.

Permissions

  • Photo library, add only (iOS). To save exports to Photos. Asked the first time you save to the gallery.
  • Storage (Android 7 to 9 only). To save exports to the gallery. Android 10 and later need no permission.

The app doesn't use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or network.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the system share sheet opens and you choose where the file goes. The app only hands over the file you picked.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version ships with an app update and is published here with a new date. Questions: contact@ekaai.tech
